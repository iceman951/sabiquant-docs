Fixed-income instruments form the bedrock of institutional quantitative finance. SabiQuant provides an analytical bond valuation engine built on `std` with zero external dependencies and numerical parity verified against QuantLib.

## Mathematical Formulation

### 1. Dirty Price Valuation

For a bond with cash flows $A_i$ (coupons and face value redemption) payable at times $t_i$ (measured in years from settlement according to the chosen day-count convention), the dirty (full) price $P(y)$ given a compounded annual yield to maturity $y$ with compounding frequency $m$ is expressed as:

$$P(y) = \sum_{i=1}^n A_i \, d(y, t_i)$$

where the discount factor $d(y, t_i)$ depends on the compounding convention:

- **Periodic Compounding ($m$ times per year):**
  $$d(y, t_i) = \left( 1 + \frac{y}{m} \right)^{-m \cdot t_i}$$

- **Continuous Compounding:**
  $$d(y, t_i) = e^{-y \cdot t_i}$$

### 2. Clean Price and Accrued Interest

Market trading conventions quote bond prices as **Clean Price** ($P_{\text{clean}}$), removing the accrued interest ($AI$) that accumulates between coupon payment dates:

$$P_{\text{dirty}} = P_{\text{clean}} + AI$$

Accrued interest is computed from the previous coupon date $t_{\text{prev}}$, settlement date $t_{\text{settle}}$, and next coupon date $t_{\text{next}}$:

$$AI = C \times \frac{\text{DayCount}(t_{\text{prev}}, t_{\text{settle}})}{\text{DayCount}(t_{\text{prev}}, t_{\text{next}})}$$

where $C$ is the gross coupon payment for the period.

### 3. Analytical Risk Measures

Rather than relying on finite-difference approximations ($\Delta y \approx 1\text{bp}$) which introduce numerical truncation error, SabiQuant calculates **exact analytical derivatives**:

$$D_{\text{mod}} = -\frac{1}{P} \frac{dP}{dy}, \quad C = \frac{1}{P} \frac{d^2P}{dy^2}$$

#### First Derivative ($dP/dy$) and Modified Duration
$$\frac{dP}{dy} = -\sum_{i=1}^n t_i \, A_i \left( 1 + \frac{y}{m} \right)^{-(m \cdot t_i + 1)}$$

$$D_{\text{mac}} = \frac{1}{P} \sum_{i=1}^n t_i \, A_i \, d(y, t_i)$$

$$D_{\text{mod}} = \frac{D_{\text{mac}}}{1 + \frac{y}{m}}$$

#### Second Derivative ($d^2P/dy^2$) and Convexity
$$\frac{d^2P}{dy^2} = \sum_{i=1}^n t_i \left( t_i + \frac{1}{m} \right) A_i \left( 1 + \frac{y}{m} \right)^{-(m \cdot t_i + 2)}$$

$$C = \frac{1}{P} \frac{d^2P}{dy^2}$$

<Aside type="tip">
  By computing $dP/dy$ and $d^2P/dy^2$ analytically, SabiQuant delivers machine-precision Taylor series expansions for bond price shifts:
  $$\frac{\Delta P}{P} \approx -D_{\text{mod}} \Delta y + \frac{1}{2} C (\Delta y)^2$$
</Aside>

## Practical Rust Example

Below is a complete, production-ready example demonstrating how to construct a `BondPricingPlan` and extract pricing and risk metrics.

```rust
use sabiquant::bonds::{BondPricingPlan, BondPricingResult};
use sabiquant::core::{Date, Frequency};
use sabiquant::core::calendars::TargetCalendar;
use sabiquant::core::daycount::ActualActualICMA;

fn main() -> Result<(), Box<dyn std::error::Error>> {
    // 1. Establish settlement date and bond maturity
    let settlement = Date::from_ymd(2025, 6, 15)?;
    let maturity   = Date::from_ymd(2035, 6, 15)?;

    // 2. Instantiate market calendar and day-count convention
    let calendar = TargetCalendar::default();
    let day_counter = ActualActualICMA::default();

    // 3. Build the BondPricingPlan
    // 10-year 5.00% semi-annual bond, par = 100.0
    let plan = BondPricingPlan::builder()
        .settlement(settlement)
        .maturity(maturity)
        .coupon_rate(0.050) // 5.00%
        .frequency(Frequency::SemiAnnual)
        .calendar(calendar)
        .day_counter(day_counter)
        .face_value(100.0)
        .build()?;

    // 4. Calculate pricing and risk at 4.75% yield
    let yield_to_maturity = 0.0475;
    let metrics: BondPricingResult = plan.price_from_yield(yield_to_maturity)?;

    println!("================ BOND VALUATION ================");
    println!("Dirty Price:        ${:.5}", metrics.dirty_price);
    println!("Clean Price:        ${:.5}", metrics.clean_price);
    println!("Accrued Interest:   ${:.5}", metrics.accrued_interest);
    println!("Macaulay Duration:  {:.4} years", metrics.macaulay_duration);
    println!("Modified Duration:  {:.4}", metrics.modified_duration);
    println!("Convexity:          {:.4}", metrics.convexity);
    println!("dP/dy (DV01 base):  {:.4}", metrics.dp_dy);
    println!("================================================");

    // 5. Inverting Yield from Market Clean Price
    // Using Brent's robust root-finding method under the hood
    let market_clean_price = 101.95;
    let implied_ytm = plan.yield_from_clean_price(market_clean_price)?;
    println!("Implied Market YTM: {:.4}%", implied_ytm * 100.0);

    Ok(())
}
```

## Output Metrics Reference

| Metric | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `dirty_price` | `f64` | Full settlement price including accrued coupon ($P_{\text{dirty}}$) |
| `clean_price` | `f64` | Quoted market price excluding accrued coupon ($P_{\text{clean}}$) |
| `accrued_interest` | `f64` | Accrued coupon earned from last payment date to settlement |
| `macaulay_duration` | `f64` | Weighted average maturity of cash flows in years ($D_{\text{mac}}$) |
| `modified_duration` | `f64` | Percentage price sensitivity per unit change in yield ($D_{\text{mod}}$) |
| `convexity` | `f64` | Second derivative curvature measure ($C$) |
| `dp_dy` | `f64` | Direct analytical first derivative $\frac{dP}{dy}$ |

<Aside type="note">
  All calculations in SabiQuant are strictly allocation-free during evaluation runs, making `BondPricingPlan` ideal for batch pricing tens of thousands of bond portfolios in ultra-low latency trading pipelines.
</Aside>
